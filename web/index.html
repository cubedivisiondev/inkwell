How to shoot it

Flat light beats bright light. Window light on an overcast day is ideal. Direct sun casts a hard gradient across the page. Do not use flash, which blows the highlight and flattens the very ramp the method depends on.

Shoot square to the page and fill the frame. Perspective is not corrected, and more pixels on the stroke make a better edge. Any paper works. Grain, texture and tint are all handled.

Nothing is uploaded. There is no server and no account. The photograph is decoded in this tab and discarded when it closes. A signature is a credential, and credentials should not travel. The fonts on this page are served from this origin for the same reason.
