WHAT IT DOES

INKWELL lifts ink off paper. Photograph a signature, a sketch, a stamp or a page of handwriting, and it returns the marks alone on a transparent background. You choose the color they come out in, so one photo of a black marker gives you a white signature for a dark card and a gold one for foil. Download it as PNG or WEBP. It runs entirely in your browser.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. DROP Photograph the page and drag the picture in, or paste it.
  2. IT READS THE LIGHT INKWELL measures how the paper was lit and divides that away, so an unevenly lit photo does not come back gray on one side.
  3. TAKE THE FILE Pick a color and a file type. The download is ready the moment you choose.

WHY IT STILL LOOKS LIKE INK

Most background removers threshold the image. They keep the dark pixels and discard the rest, which is a hard cut, and the hard cut takes the soft edge of every stroke with it. It also takes the swell where the pen pressed harder and the skip where a dry marker lifted off the page. What comes back is a flat silhouette. A flat silhouette reads as clip art, and it reads that way immediately.

INKWELL never thresholds. The grayscale of your photograph becomes the transparency directly, so the soft edges survive into the file you download. That is the whole difference.

FAQ

How do I get a transparent PNG of my signature?
Sign a sheet of paper, photograph it, and drop the photo in. INKWELL returns the ink alone with a real alpha channel. Choose black, white, gold, silver, red, blue, or type any hex code.
Why does my signature look fake in other tools?
They threshold it. A hard cut flattens the stroke and throws away the edge quality that makes handwriting look handwritten. INKWELL keeps the full grayscale ramp as the transparency instead.
My photo is lit unevenly. Does that matter?
No. INKWELL measures how the paper was lit and divides that out, so a gradient across the sheet does not leave one half gray and the other blown out. Flat window light still gives the cleanest result, and flash gives the worst.
Is anything uploaded?
No. INKWELL runs entirely in your browser. The photo is read from disk, processed on your machine, and gone when the tab closes. A signature is a credential. It does not travel.
Can I use a color that is not in the presets?
Yes. Type any six digit hex code. The color is applied through the extracted matte rather than sampled from the photo, so one shot of black marker produces a clean mark in whatever color you name.
Can I get a vector for foil, letterpress or a cutting machine?
Yes, from the command line tool. Those processes are driven by a plate or a blade, so the machine needs a closed outline rather than pixels. Install it with pip install inkwell-ink and pass the trace flag. Vector export from this page is not finished yet.
